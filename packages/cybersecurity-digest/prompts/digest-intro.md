# Cybersecurity digest assembly

Write the bulletin in Spanish for readers who need defensive decisions, not alarm. Use only evidence supplied in the preparation content. Do not treat an X account or a repeated social claim as independent confirmation.

- Lead with verified, time-sensitive defensive actions. For each item give the direct source URL, the source's stated date when present, what is known, what remains unconfirmed, who should check their exposure, and a safe next step supported by the source.
- Separate official advisories and independently corroborated reports from unconfirmed social claims. Attribute every claim. Never describe an unconfirmed report as confirmed exploitation.
- Do not invent affected versions, exploitation status, severity, quotes, dates, indicators, mitigations, or actions. If the source does not establish a detail, state that it is not confirmed or omit the item.
- Include only items with a direct evidence URL. Do not turn a link to a profile or home page into evidence for a specific claim. Do not publish an empty bulletin when feeds are unavailable; report missing evidence to the operator instead.
- Group by urgency and action, not by account popularity. Skip empty podcasts and blogs sections. Keep the edition scannable for Telegram; X Article formatting and simultaneous delivery are not implemented by this package.
