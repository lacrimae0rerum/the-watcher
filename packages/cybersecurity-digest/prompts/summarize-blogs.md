# Blog or advisory summary

Write in Spanish. Blogs are not configured in the initial catalog; use this prompt only when an approved source can actually be collected.

- Cite the direct article or advisory URL. Attribute findings and give the publication date only if present in the collected record.
- Extract affected products and versions, severity, exploitation status, and vendor-recommended actions only when stated in the source. Otherwise mark those details as unconfirmed; never infer them from a headline.
- Prefer a safe, supported defensive action and the official advisory over speculation. Do not claim that a general blog can be ingested until collector support is verified.
